Two errors, added.

The Earth leans, and its orbit is not a circle. Neither fault is large. Together they move noon by half an hour.

The leanThe axis is tilted 23.4°, so the sun's apparent progress along the horizon speeds up and slows down through the year. This curve runs from −9.9 to +9.9 minutes and does it twice.

The ellipseThe orbit is not circular, so the Earth travels faster in January than in July. One slow swing a year, −7.1 to +7.1 minutes.

The equation of timeAdd them. The sum reaches +16.5 minutes on 3 November and −14.2 on 11 February. Your clock ignores all of it.
